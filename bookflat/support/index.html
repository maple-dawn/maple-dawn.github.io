常见问题

书页和文字会上传吗？ 不会。拍摄、裁剪、文字识别都在 iPhone 上完成，开飞行模式也能用。

双页书怎么拆成两页？ 拍摄时选「书本」模式，把书脊对准中线；在「展平与裁剪」页打开「拆分双页」即可。

识别出的文字在哪里？ 打开文档，点底部的「识字」，或点开某一页后切换到「文本」，可以一键复制。

导出的 PDF 为什么有水印？ 免费版导出会带一行“拍书”小水印。试用期间或永久解锁后，导出不带水印，并可生成可搜索 PDF、导出 TXT。

怎么恢复购买？ 首页右上角「设置」→「恢复购买」。

试用到期会扣款吗？ 不会。7 天试用结束后不会自动扣款，需要你主动购买才会永久解锁。

删除 App 后文档还在吗？ 不在。文档只保存在本机，删除前请先导出需要保留的内容。

隐私政策

Need help?

For problems, recognition issues or feature requests, please open an issue on GitHub. Please don't attach photos or screenshots that contain real personal information.

Are my pages or text uploaded? No. Capturing, cropping and text recognition all run on your iPhone, even in Airplane Mode.

How do I split a two-page spread? Use Book mode and line the spine up with the center guide, then turn on “Split pages” on the crop screen.

Where is the recognized text? Open a document and tap “Text” at the bottom, or open a page and switch to the Text tab. You can copy it all at once.

Why does my exported PDF have a watermark? The free version adds a small “BookFlat” watermark. During the trial or after the lifetime unlock, exports have no watermark, and you can create searchable PDFs and export TXT.

How do I restore my purchase? Tap Settings at the top right of the home screen → Restore Purchase.

Will I be charged when the trial ends? No. The 7-day trial never charges automatically.

Are my documents kept if I delete the app? No. Documents are stored only on your device, so export anything you want to keep first.
