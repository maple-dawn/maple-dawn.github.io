Masaic

Hide private info in one tap.

Masaic checks your screenshots for faces, emails, card numbers, tracking numbers, QR codes and more, then masks them in one tap. Everything happens on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded.

Download on the App Store

Free download · iPhone · iPad

Features

01

Finds it for you

Faces, email addresses, bank card numbers, tracking numbers, QR codes, barcodes, IP addresses, chat avatars and nicknames. Review what it found and skip anything you want to keep.

02

Mask it your way

Pixelate, blur, marker, emoji, or Hide Text, which fills text with the surrounding background and leaves no trace.

03

Precise by hand

Tap a line to mask it, or draw a box around anything. Adjust the strength and set your default style.

04

Watermark your ID

Cover ID cards and passports with a watermark like “For rental application only” so they can’t be reused.

05

Clean metadata

Location, camera model and other EXIF data are removed when you share.

06

Right from the Share menu

Mask an image straight from the Share menu in Photos or any other app.

Private by design

Detection runs entirely on device and works in Airplane Mode. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking — your images never leave your iPhone.

Pricing

Try every feature free for 7 days, then unlock forever with a one-time purchase. No subscription and no automatic charge. Manual selection, solid blocks and EXIF removal stay free.
