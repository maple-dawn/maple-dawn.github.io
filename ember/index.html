Ember for Hacker News

A calm, fast way to read Hacker News.

Ember is a quiet Hacker News reader for iPhone and iPad, with collapsible threads, offline reading and on-device AI summaries.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free download · iPhone · iPad

Features

01

Threads that make sense

Collapse long discussions and follow replies without losing your place.

02

Offline reading

Stories and articles download in the background over Wi-Fi, ready to read anywhere.

03

On-device AI summaries

Summarize articles and comments with Apple’s on-device model. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

04

Keyword filters

Hide stories about topics you don’t want to see.

05

Vote and reply

Optionally log in to Hacker News to vote, comment and reply. Your password is never stored.

06

Widgets

Top stories on your Home Screen, in your theme color.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Saved stories, history and filters stay on your device, and the developer runs no servers.
