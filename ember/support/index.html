常见问题

免费试用怎么算？ 点“免费试用 7 天”即可在 7 天内使用全部 Premium 功能。试用不会扣款；结束后 Premium 功能会锁定，直到你购买一次性解锁。你的过滤规则和设置都会保留。

怎么恢复购买？ 用同一个 Apple 账户登录，打开 Ember 的“设置”，点“恢复购买”。付费页上也有这个按钮。

AI 摘要显示“此 iPhone 不可用”？ 摘要使用 Apple 的设备端模型，需要 iPhone 15 Pro 或更新机型，并在“设置”中开启 Apple 智能、等待模型下载完成。该模型目前并非在所有国家或地区提供（例如中国大陆），这种情况下摘要无法使用，其他 Premium 功能不受影响。

无法登录 Hacker News？ 如果 Hacker News 要求验证你是真人，请先在 Safari 中登录一次 news.ycombinator.com，再回到 Ember 重试。

今早没有自动离线下载？ 后台下载的具体时间由 iOS 决定，低电量模式下可能会跳过；默认只在 Wi-Fi 下下载。你随时可以在“设置 → 离线阅读”中点“立即下载”。

隐私政策

Need help?

For problems or feature requests, please open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How does the free trial work? Tap “Try free for 7 days” to use every Premium feature for 7 days. The trial never charges you; when it ends, Premium features lock until you buy the one-time unlock. Your filters and settings are kept.

How do I restore my purchase? Signed in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Ember and tap “Restore purchase”. It’s also on the paywall.

AI summaries say “Not available on this iPhone”? Summaries use Apple’s on-device model. It needs iPhone 15 Pro or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings and finished downloading. The model isn’t offered in every country or region yet (for example mainland China); there, summaries can’t run, and every other Premium feature still works.

I can’t log in to Hacker News. If Hacker News asks to verify you’re human, log in once at news.ycombinator.com in Safari, then try again in Ember.

Offline downloads didn’t run this morning. iOS decides when background downloads run and may skip them in Low Power Mode. By default they also only use Wi-Fi. You can always tap “Download now” in Settings › Offline reading.
