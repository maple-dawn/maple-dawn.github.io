Transory

Compare AI translations side by side.

Transory runs on the AI models you choose. Add your own API keys, send the same text to several models at once, and pick the translation that reads best.

Download on the App Store

Free download · iPhone · iPad

Features

01

Multi-model comparison

Each model gets its own card, streamed in real time: DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Kimi, Qwen and more.

02

Bring your own model

Use any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoint, or Ollama and LM Studio on your own network.

03

Live translation

Live bilingual captions for talks and meetings, and a split-screen conversation mode with translations read aloud.

04

Photo translation

Snap a menu, sign or document. Text is recognized on device and translations are laid over the original.

05

Natural style

Choose Natural, Formal, Casual or Literal. The source language is detected automatically.

06

History & favorites

Your history stays on your device. Search it, star favorites and delete anytime.

Private by design

Transory has no servers and collects no data. Before anything is sent, you see what goes where and authorize each service yourself. API keys are stored in the system Keychain.
