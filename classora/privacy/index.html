简体中文

Classora: Class Schedule & Timetable（以下称“本应用”）是一个 iPhone 课表应用。我们的原则很简单：本应用不收集、不存储、不分享你的任何个人数据。没有账号系统、没有广告、没有统计分析，也没有任何追踪。开发者不运营任何接收你信息的服务器。

1. 你的课表保存在哪里

你输入的课程、教室、老师、作息时间、学期、假期、单次调课、作业与考试，以及各项设置，都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。本应用不会把这些内容上传到任何地方。删除本应用即会一并删除这些数据。

2. 网络连接

本应用的课表功能完全离线运行，不需要网络。唯一的网络连接是你购买或恢复 Premium 时，由 iOS 与 Apple 的 App Store 通信。

3. 通知

上课提醒和试用到期提醒都是在你的设备上安排的本地通知，不经过任何服务器。你可以随时在 iOS“设置”或本应用中关闭。

4. 小组件与实时活动

主屏幕和锁定屏幕小组件、实时活动（锁定屏幕与灵动岛上的下课倒计时）通过本应用的共享存储（App Group）在本机读取接下来几天的课程。这些数据不会离开你的设备。

5. 购买

“7 天免费试用”和“永久解锁”通过 Apple 的 App Store 内购完成。试用结束后不会自动扣款。付款与购买记录由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。我们无法获取你的姓名、付款信息或 Apple ID。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用面向学生，可能被未成年人使用。本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

7. 政策变更

如果本政策有更新，我们会修改本页面并更新上方的生效日期。如果将来的版本需要收集任何数据（例如加入 iCloud 同步），我们会在应用内明确告知并征得你的同意。

8. 联系我们

如有任何问题，请在这里留言：github.com/maple-dawn/maple-dawn.github.io/issues，或发邮件至 maple.indawn@gmail.com。

English

Classora: Class Schedule & Timetable (“the app”) is a class timetable app for iPhone. Our principle is simple: the app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers that receive information about you.

1. Where your timetable is stored

The classes, rooms, teachers, bell times, terms, holidays, one-off class changes, homework and exams you enter, and all your settings, are stored only on your iPhone. The app never uploads them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

2. Network access

The timetable works entirely offline and needs no network. The only connection is between iOS and Apple’s App Store when you buy or restore Premium.

3. Notifications

Class reminders and the trial-ending reminder are local notifications scheduled on your device; they don’t pass through any server. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings or in the app.

4. Widgets and Live Activities

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity (the class countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island) read the next few days of classes on device, through the app’s shared storage (App Group). This data never leaves your device.

5. Purchases

The 7-day free trial and the lifetime unlock are App Store in-app purchases. The trial never turns into an automatic charge. Payment and purchase records are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is made for students and may be used by minors. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version ever needs to collect any data (for example, to add iCloud sync), we will tell you clearly in the app and ask for your consent first.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us at github.com/maple-dawn/maple-dawn.github.io/issues or email maple.indawn@gmail.com.

本アプリは学生向けで、未成年の方が利用する場合があります。本アプリは、子どもを含むいかなる方の個人情報も収集しません。

本ポリシーを変更する場合は、このページと上記の施行日を更新します。将来のバージョンでデータの収集が必要になる場合（例：iCloud 同期の追加）は、アプリ内で明確にお知らせし、事前に同意をいただきます。
