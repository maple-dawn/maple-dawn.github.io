Classora

Your school day, at a glance.

Set up your timetable once, then see what’s on now, what’s next and which room to go to. Classora handles the messy parts of a real school week.

Free download · iPhone · iPad

Features

01

Now and next

A live countdown to the end of class, the rest of your day, and room changes clearly marked.

02

Your week

A clean weekly grid, with Week A/B rotations, holidays and make-up days worked out automatically.

03

One-off changes

Change a single class just once: cancelled, a different room or a substitute teacher.

04

Built for your school

Fixed bell times for school or custom times for university, with double periods and a color for every class.

05

Never miss a class

A reminder before each class, with room changes included. Cancelled classes are skipped.

06

Widgets & Live Activity

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, plus a class countdown in the Dynamic Island (Premium).

Private by design

Your timetable stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
